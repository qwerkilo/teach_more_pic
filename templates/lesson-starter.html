第一幕 Act I

设置矛盾

Setting the Conflict

传统经济学认为政府像家庭一样需要量入为出。但 MMT 指出：拥有主权货币的国家（如美日英）不存在"破产"概念——它们总能通过创造新货币来偿还本币债务。

Traditional economics treats government like a household that must balance its budget. MMT argues: sovereign currency issuers (like the US, Japan, UK) cannot "go bankrupt"—they can always create new money to repay debt in their own currency.

核心洞察：主权货币发行国 ≠ 家庭。税收不是为了"筹钱"，而是为了控制通胀。

Key Insight: Sovereign currency issuers ≠ households. Taxes aren't about "raising money"—they regulate inflation.

第二幕 Act II

危机爆发

Crisis Erupts

2008 年金融危机后，各国央行大规模量化宽松（QE），但并未引发预期中的恶性通胀。为什么？因为新增的货币停留在金融体系内，没有流入实体经济——这就是 MMT 的"部门余额"理论。

After the 2008 financial crisis, central banks launched massive QE programs without triggering expected hyperinflation. Why? Because new money stayed in the financial system, not the real economy—the "sectoral balances" theory of MMT.

第三幕 Act III

转折与遗产

Turning Point & Legacy

MMT 的真正遗产不在于"印钞"，而在于重新定义财政空间：只要经济还有闲置资源（失业、闲置产能），政府就可以借债支出。这为下一课《就业保障计划》埋下了伏笔。

MMT's real legacy isn't about "printing money"—it redefines fiscal space: as long as the economy has idle resources (unemployment, spare capacity), government can spend. This sets the stage for the next lesson on Job Guarantee programs.

关键问题：如果政府真的不需要税收来"买单"，为什么还要征税？Key question: If government doesn't need taxes to "pay for" spending, why tax at all?

财政部在央行开设账户，签发支票即创造货币。

The Treasury creates money by writing checks from its central bank account.

货币在经济体中流转，形成购买力。

Money circulates in the economy, creating purchasing power.

税收从经济体中回收货币，防止过热。

Taxes withdraw money from the economy to prevent overheating.

"赤字是结果，不是原因。财政余额是私人部门净储蓄的镜像。"

"The deficit is a result, not a cause. Fiscal balances are the mirror of private sector net saving."

Stephanie Kelton

政府必须像家庭一样平衡预算；赤字会导致通货膨胀和债务危机。

Government must balance its budget like a household; deficits cause inflation and debt crises.

主权货币发行国不受预算硬约束；赤字是私人部门净储蓄的正常结果。

Sovereign issuers face no hard budget constraint; deficits are the normal result of private sector net saving.

MMT 核心公式：政府赤字 = 私人部门盈余 + 贸易赤字。这不是巧合，而是会计恒等式。

MMT Core Identity: Gov Deficit = Private Sector Surplus + Trade Deficit. This is not a coincidence—it's an accounting identity.
